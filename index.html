Introduction to Python Programming

Welcome! These are the lessons for CSCI 1220: Introduction to Python Programming at Central New Mexico Community College. The course is for people who have never programmed before.

Work through the modules in order. Each module has short lessons with examples to type yourself, a reading from Python Unlocked, and a ready-to-run Google Colab notebook. Your graded programs and due dates are in Brightspace.
